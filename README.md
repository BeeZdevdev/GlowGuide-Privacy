# PRIVACY POLICY FOR GLOWGUIDE

Last updated: May 20, 2026

**Overview**

GlowGuide ("the app") is a personal habit and goal tracking app. This policy explains how your data is handled.

**Data We Collect**

We do not collect any personal data. The app does not transmit any information to us or any third party.

**Data Stored on Your Device**

All data you enter, including your goal name, categories, activity records, completion dates, and preferences, is stored locally on your device only. On Android, data is saved as JSON files in your device's external storage under /GlowGuide/. This data never leaves your device.

**Third-Party Services**

The app does not use any analytics, advertising, crash reporting, or other third-party services that collect data.

**Internet Permission**

The app does not make any network requests.

**Data Deletion**

To delete all app data, uninstall the app and manually delete the /GlowGuide/ folder from your device storage.

**Children's Privacy**

The app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

**Changes to This Policy**

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted with a new effective date.
